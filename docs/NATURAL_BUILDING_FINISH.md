# Natural TES building finish (beta78 / architecture revision 20)

Fresh standalone fits omit optional external yards. A single flush grass/sand footing remains
at the approach, rather than the old three-wide snow/cobblestone strip. Optional decorated
projects keep complete side/rear features and their connected circulation. Essential building
floors, porches, steps, native fixtures and door access are not optional terrain decorations.

New blueprint foundations use dirt or desert sandstone. Bounded hillside earthwork caps use
nearby podzol, mycelium, grass, snow or unpolished rock, with solid non-falling backing.
There is no blanket deletion/repainting of a neighboring structure or a live world's old pad.

Door conversion surveys the compartment at head height, with doors/glass closed. Only an
entrance with open outside-connected compartments on both sides becomes a matching wooden gate.
Gates start open because vanilla villagers cannot operate closed gates. Enclosed buildings,
iron doors and exact imported vanilla templates do not change. Snow drifts are deterministic
one-to-four layers on exposed supported wooden roof courses; fixtures, access hatches and
campfire flues are not capped. Of the 75 snowy designs, 73 have multiple varied drift heights;
one has a single supported snow tile, and the guard blockhouse keeps its usable roof platform clear.

Affected-tree discovery happens while the original tree still exists. A clipped leaf can find
its nearby natural trunk. The frozen receipt includes connected natural logs and bounded
associated leaves; foliage equally close or closer to an untouched tree remains. Snow caps
and dependent hanging vines can join the receipt. The receipt is replayed before construction
completion, with normal protection checks at every write. Unloaded/oversized crowns wait or
reject the new candidate instead of being partially cleared. No radius-based deforestation,
crafted timber frames, persistent landscaping or recorded planted-tree clearing is allowed.

All historical architecture revisions are retained. Older clearing receipts cannot be expanded
safely after their original tree evidence is gone. Already-finished buildings are not remodeled
automatically, and no live save was edited during implementation. Use `/tesstructure` to identify
an older affected building before requesting a targeted, ownership-aware repair.

Handbook review: guided Building ID explains the behavior, safety and historical limits;
the compact Building ID page summarizes gates, drifts and canopy clearing. Recipe routing,
animations and server definitions remain unchanged: no new item or recipe was added.
