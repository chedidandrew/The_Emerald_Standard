## Improvements confirmed

### News: previous logic problems addressed, fuller newsroom identity

NewsWire.choose now derives the most recent archive occurrence of each headline rather than repeatedly selecting its oldest occurrence. Roundups resolve the publisher once and pass it consistently into narrative generation. Player reports carry exact subjects and source article IDs; restocking can link to the same store without linking a different store in the same village. The previous generic food follow-up for structure damage is no longer emitted by that path.

NewsNarrative, NewsColumns and NewsFeatures add developed prose, five recurring correspondents, factual notebook sections, and 24 rotating feature entries. NewsCivic observes project milestones and restoration without creating a false history on its first observation. Market continuations require meaningful new price information, retain the original source link, and do not claim that price recovery proves physical repair. NewsReader introduces local relevance and topic/outlet diversity while retaining the bounded archive and stable edition behavior.

A controlled sequence of 72 upward roundups produced 12 distinct roundup headlines with zero consecutive headline repetitions and consistent masthead/byline/column attribution. This isolates the former rotation/voice defects; it is not a natural-market occurrence-frequency test.

Editorial limitation: generated roundup samples still repeat substantial outlet-specific openings, common consequence paragraphs and endings. Recurring characters are an improvement, but a rotated notebook paragraph is not always closely connected to the precise event. The next writing pass should vary complete story structures, use shorter briefs, and tie reactions to concrete observed developments instead of simply increasing headline count.

### Districts: shared work budget, smaller finishing contexts and better composition

ConstructionWorkBudget now provides shared admission with at most 64 reserved operations and four job batches per pulse, at most 16 operations per batch, plus a cooperative four-millisecond admission window. It rotates access between Bank and village lanes. This is not a hard guarantee that a tick finishes within four milliseconds: native operations are non-preemptible, and the first bounded batch may be admitted after an expensive preflight so it does not starve permanently.

VillageProsperityManager uses VillageFinishingQueue.view for outstanding finishing work instead of the previously identified whole-village copy for each completed building. Nearby identity resolution and limited snapshot admission further reduce unnecessary copying. Some whole-dimension exclusion gathering and synchronous whole-economy financial saves remain; a global admission object alone does not establish measured large-world latency.

Organic site candidates now include sub-parcel positions, compact infill and ranked connected edges. The probe's seeded territory produced 285 candidate positions, 228 of them not at parcel centers. This verifies that the former center-only restriction is gone; it does not prove that every terrain arrangement has an attractive or safe solution.

Organic mature housing selection now mixes Houses and Cottages and admits occasional Inns only with appropriate demand/composition. In a controlled tier-four fixture without visitor/trade demand, 100 project serials selected 65 Houses and 35 Cottages, with no Inns. This sample is not a promised distribution for actual gameplay. Legacy districts retain their prior compatibility behavior.

Builder cues now respond to recent material work and occasional inspection. Town guidance better distinguishes waiting, inspection and ordinary construction. Shared paths, Bank connections, lamps and protected footbridges strengthen the relationship between buildings rather than leaving each structure isolated.

### Minecraft-native integration

The existing 52 authored TES blueprint descriptors remain. The new vanilla catalog adds 166 curated template entries: 151 ordinary buildings/agricultural structures/enclosures and 15 occasional civic spaces across five default village families. Those are catalog counts, not a claim of 218 newly visually reviewed buildings.

The original natural-village family governs imports across biome boundaries. Imported housing uses actual beds. Plans are frozen before construction and use existing ownership, occupancy, protection and finishing paths. Imported containers begin empty, crops begin young, and template entity data does not supply free residents or animals. Exact audited template hashes constrain resource replacement. This is vanilla integration, not universal modded-village support.

The beta.45 validation record describes exhaustive local flat-site construction fixtures for the 166 imports. Those historical fixture results are useful but do not override the failed exact beta.48 runtime workflow or certify all terrain, shader and multiplayer combinations.

### Geographic map and practical fixes

The beta.47 map replaces hidden marker-page navigation with a continuous geographic viewport, Focus/Overview behavior and bounded regional summaries that resolve into individual sites as the view narrows. Panning does not authorize world chunk loading.

Beta.48 addresses natural dirt/grass transitions during finishing checks, shallow foundations over superflat bedrock, repeated rejected-site work, and accelerated-mode survey/arrival behavior. The new default monthly Fund spending allowance is 240 emeralds; explicit existing settings are preserved. These targeted repairs are more valuable than raising raw construction speed while leaving the causes of stalls unexplained.

### Dedicated servers: a concrete occupied-terrain assertion, not the previous timeout

Inspected artifacts: Fabric server `10331367614` and NeoForge server `10331886016`.

Both logs contain:

```text
IllegalStateException: Could not start The Emerald Standard economy
Caused by: IllegalStateException: Village expansion runtime check failed
Caused by: IllegalStateException: terrain records a temporary entity wait
```

The failing assertion is in VillageExpansionSelfTest.verifyFirstDistrict, around line 310. The preceding assertion checks preserved footing, unchanged reservation and no inappropriate failure/relocation accounting; execution reaches the subsequent waiting-status assertion. The logs therefore do not justify asserting that the mod built through a player or destroyed protected property.

These servers ran with the explicit integrationSmoke property. Diagnose whether the failure is in wait-state reporting/admission or a fixture assuming progress in a particular pulse. Do not claim that ordinary user startup necessarily fails from this test exception, and do not dismiss the failed test as harmless without isolating it. The old beta.31 180-second watchdog failure is not the failure reported by this exact run.

Several earlier native construction fixtures pass before the assertion, including the specific historical Cottage/Inn support-recovery cases. Partial fixture passes do not make the overall server suite green.

### Clients: first animation failures and a dependent restart failure

Inspected artifacts: Fabric with Mod Menu `10331616868`, and NeoForge client `10331641651`.

In the first Fabric process, the cursor and reader checks progress before ClientSmokeSupport fails with:

```text
IllegalStateException: Recipe animation did not advance
```

The first NeoForge process instead reaches:

```text
IllegalStateException: Hover did not pause recipe animation
```

Both later restart processes report:

```text
AssertionError: reader preference did not survive process restart
```

ClientSmokeSupport saves the intended final 110-percent preference only near the successful end of the first process, after the failing animation assertions. Its exception handler logs the failure and stops Minecraft, which can leave Gradle's process status successful. The shell loop advances to the restart on that status and does not inspect the accumulated log until after both processes.

Consequently, the restart assertion is not independent evidence that correctly saved preferences are lost. The first phase never reached its required setup point in these logs. A separate local two-process test of ReaderPreferences saved and loaded 110 successfully, but that does not validate the complete Minecraft settings UI.

Recommended correction: isolate the first animation/hover failure with deterministic input and render state, preserve genuine failure detection, and require a first-phase success marker or explicit failure exit before launching the restart phase. Do not merely weaken animation assertions or whitelist all errors. The inspected cursor probes pass; repeating the old X11 diagnosis would be misleading.

## Additional local test inconsistency

The packaged auxiliary EconomySelfTest was also executed, unmodified, and **failed**:

```text
Exit code: 1
AssertionError: Implausible VILX CAGR: -0.009328487932914409
```

This test compounds EconomyEngine.marketReturn directly for 100 years at seed 42 and expects a 2-22 percent compound annual growth range. Its measured factor CAGR was approximately -0.93 percent.

Do not interpret that as proof of the actual current VILX investment's return. The current live market advances constituent prices and calls StockIndex.reprice; the standalone test does not exercise that complete path. Its own class comment describes it as a minimal standalone smoke test separate from the full suite. Reconcile this helper with the model it is meant to validate, and use current full-path index/market regressions for economic behavior. Do not tune investment returns merely to satisfy a potentially outdated proxy test.
