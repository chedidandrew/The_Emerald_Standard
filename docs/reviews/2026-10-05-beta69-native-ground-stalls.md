- Interpret only ground-level required phase-7 TES paths whose immutable saved plan has a required native-solid cover as stable dirt footing. Native fence gates are excluded. Never infer this permission from world obstructions or player-created covers. Vanilla-exact templates and arbitrary required floors are not reinterpreted.
- Keep original authored block states, canonical hashes, geometry assets, schedule indices, saved cuts, receipts and total counts. Annotate the cached runtime plan after canonical hash verification. Placement, native survival fallback, protection and occupancy use the stable state. Support matching and physical finishing accept the same natural dirt/grass footing. Missing footing still needs safe placement; masonry, storage, fluids, protected ground and unloaded cells are not waived.
- Retain exact-cell no-drop receipts when supplied dirt/grass changes naturally, including a supplied covered path becoming dirt and then grass. Unowned natural terrain and mere reservations do not gain drop ownership. Handover still removes repair authority and permits ordinary drops.
- Debug pending cells now expose authored and effective construction states, the planned covered-path flag and the actual overhead block, alongside existing cursor/role/survival evidence.
