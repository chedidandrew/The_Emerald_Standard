# Beta80 — connected entrance staircases

Use the binary JAR for your loader; the sources JAR is for development only.
SHA256SUMS accompanies each pair. No installed profile or live world was changed.

New raised TES entrances attach to the actual rotated/mirrored outside exit. A straight,
three-block-wide flight descends to a grounded bottom landing, with matching solid
backing instead of detached narrow stairs or floating steps. Road bends start after
the flight. Flat entrances gain no extra stairs or apron. Fresh Banks and exact-vanilla
copies use the exterior access planner without changing their authored buildings.

The whole flight is surveyed and frozen with terrain work before writes. Existing player
blocks, storage, claims, fluids and unloaded chunks still prevent unsafe placement.
Changed required stairs/footing prevent project handover; they do not authorize repair
over a player edit. Existing saved plans and completed older stairs are not remodeled.

Includes beta79 stone retaining courses and beta78 natural ground/roof/tree improvements.
Architecture remains revision 20. No new items or recipes. Both handbook forms updated.
See docs/reviews/2026-10-07-beta80-entrance-staircases.md for validation details.
