# Beta82 — bounded stale-construction recovery

Adds a rotating sweep of later approved work when timed support recovery is active. The earliest pending cell is checked every visit and remains the saved progress boundary until resolved. A plan longer than the per-visit scan cap no longer repeatedly inspects only the same first segment during recovery.

Recovery hints are temporary, per unfinished site and upgrade suffix. They survive eligible episode expiry, but reset on real progress, hard safety waits and inactivity; restarts begin normally. Native survival, protection, occupancy, fluid, loaded-chunk and completion checks are not bypassed. Frozen architecture assets, hashes and operation totals are unchanged. This cannot guarantee recovery from unknown defects or genuine unsafe sites.

Both handbook forms explain the fallback and its limits. No items, recipes, live-world edits or automatic installation.
