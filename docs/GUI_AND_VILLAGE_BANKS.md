# Banker GUI and village banks

## Player experience

The Emerald Standard is designed as a natural village service rather than a command console.

1. Read the one-time Starter Handbook placed in your inventory, then follow its discovery hint and visit an Overworld village. The book never opens automatically.
2. Locate the Village Bank and Exchange or a villager named Banker.
3. Right-click a Banker or any Exchange Desk. A lectern in an upgraded legacy bank remains a valid access point only at its persisted bank location.
4. On Account, choose an amount and deposit physical emeralds into Bank Cash.
5. Use the eight-page graphical dashboard. The first successful Banker visit explains the distinction between safe deposits, locked CDs, and investments that can lose value.

No command is required for ordinary banking, investing, lending, exchange, or Prosperity Fund gameplay.

## Starter Handbook

With `onboarding.join_hint_enabled=true`, the server gives each player one Starter Handbook in each world and sends the existing discovery message. The book is inserted into inventory without opening the book screen. The per-world onboarding marker is written only after the inventory accepts the item; if no slot can accept it, the handbook is not dropped and delivery is retried on that player's next join.

The handbook is a concise tutorial followed by an indexed reference. Colored headings, labeled safe/locked/risky/gift cues, compact money-flow and lifecycle diagrams, crafting layouts, and hover explanations provide visual context without requiring a custom screen. Its clickable table of contents and previous/contents/next links jump directly between sections. The pages cover getting started, exact amounts and confirmations, all eight dashboard pages and Banking subviews, the nine investments, all 18 Trade resources, Village Prosperity and safety, lifecycle recovery, the ten development projects, Planning versus Building, terrain adaptation and integrity rules, the Prosperity Fund, Activity and News, economic time and operator debugging, durable transaction recovery, common access and construction problems, recipes, and a finance and settlement glossary.

Handbooks are renewable. A normal Book and one Emerald craft a replacement in any arrangement, and obtaining either ingredient reveals the shapeless recipe. Crafted copies contain the same current visual guide and clickable navigation as the first-join copy. The recipe remains available when automatic onboarding is disabled.

## Dashboard pages

The dashboard preserves a collision-tested 320x230 logical layout but responsively grows or shrinks within safe bounds for the current window. Custom labels keep Minecraft's native glyph size to match button and EditBox text while their anchors, clipping widths, panels, and hitboxes scale. Supporting explanations wrap in compact hover tooltips. Hovering an Account balance shows its exact two-decimal value, while mature/early CD closure and disabled Fund-purpose controls explain their current state. Every amount-bearing page identifies the applied amount, and transactional controls explain their source, destination, and projected balances.

### Account

Shows net worth, Bank Cash, Savings, invested value, physical inventory emeralds, current economic day, market regime, total external contributions, realized and unrealized performance, and personal net-worth history. Quick actions explicitly deposit Inventory items into Bank Cash, withdraw Bank Cash to Inventory, open Banking transfers, or recover an interrupted journaled inventory transaction.

### Market

The previous/next carousel shows one of the nine investments at a time. The selected investment controls the current price, chart, sector, risk, shares held, holding value, portfolio allocation, average purchase price, total cost basis, market bulletin, and Buy/Sell actions. Players can buy a selected emerald amount, sell 25 percent, or sell all. Sell-all requires confirmation. The 0.25 percent per-side trading spread remains in effect.

### Banking

Uses separate Transfers, CDs, and Villager Loans subviews. Transfers show Inventory, Bank Cash, and Savings together and provide four unambiguous routes: Inventory -> Bank Cash, Bank Cash -> Inventory, Bank Cash -> Savings, and Savings -> Bank Cash. A persistent note explains that Savings must pass through Bank Cash before withdrawal or investment.

The CD and Villager Loans views show the spendable Bank Cash source, total product value, position count, selected term/position, selected amount, and projected balances after opening a position. A player can hold up to eight positions of each type; selectors choose the exact CD to close or resolved loan to collect. The Close CD tooltip distinguishes a penalty-bearing early close from a penalty-free mature close. Lending visibly warns that principal can be lost while also stating that no debt can be created. Funding lending and closing a CD require confirmation.

### Trade

Cycles through 18 supported valuable resource forms, shows the canonical vanilla resource item beside an emerald, current inventory count, dynamic emerald quote, derived quote history, selected amount, proceeds, remaining items, and resulting Bank Cash. Variant histories use the same ore/block/crafting formula as their live quote, so ingots, blocks, and ores no longer depend on nonexistent variant-specific save keys.

### Village

Shows the local settlement connected to the current bank access point:

- Lifecycle and development tier
- Productive population and housing
- Prosperity and safety
- Food, materials, and treasury
- Farming, mining, and trade output
- Current development project and visual backlog
- Distinct Planning and physical Building percentages; villagers break ground after approval and reveal more blocks as planning advances
- Simulation/visual-progression mode and local market-impact summary
- Restoration progress, or the latest local incident cause and age when restoration is not active

### Fund

Shows the associated settlement's spendable balance, protected endowment principal, emergency reserve, lifetime receipts, and the current player's lifetime support and non-financial donor title. Players choose among Direct Grant, Endowment, and Project Sponsorship. Grants and endowments may target General, Housing, Food, Infrastructure, Security, Trade, or Restoration when targeted donations are enabled. A restoration Direct Grant is fixed to Restoration, while a sponsorship follows the displayed active economically unfinished project and derives its purpose from that project's type. The disabled purpose control explains whether targeting is unavailable, restoration-fixed, or project-fixed.

The contribution amount uses the same exact typed Apply, Cancel, Enter, and `All` controls as other money flows. The applied amount is server-owned, bounded against live Bank Cash, and uses a separate packet range from ordinary transactions. The contribution button requires a second matching click within the server confirmation window. Contributions are irreversible gifts to the settlement; they are not player loans or investments and never create debt.

### Activity

Shows lifetime bank inflow and withdrawals plus a scrollable, newest-first view of the player's complete retained transaction ledger. Five entries fit at once; the mouse wheel and visible newer/older controls securely request another bounded window from the server. A cycling filter selects All, Cash & Transfers, Investments, Bank Products, Exchange, or Village Fund before the server calculates totals and paging. The position indicator covers empty, partial, and full 256-entry ledgers. Deposits, withdrawals and refunds, cash/savings transfers, buy/sell activity, CDs, lending, commodity exchange, and Prosperity Fund support have distinct labels. Buy and sell entries include their asset ticker, while new exchange entries retain the resource and item count.

### News

Shows one compact local bulletin with a Minecraft item visual, headline, wrapped article, and wrapped prosperity and safety next steps. A deterministic shared policy derives every result only from the synchronized settlement lifecycle, population, housing, supplies, scores, project, incident, output, and configuration facts. It does not call an external AI, invent an event that the server did not record, or mutate village state. Disabled simulation or on-site visual progression produces an explicit paused bulletin rather than a claim that work is advancing. Hovering the article or either guidance area reveals its full text.

## Exact transaction amounts

Banking, investing, exchange, and Fund actions use one typed whole-emerald amount from `1` through `1,000,000`, plus Apply, Cancel, Enter, and `All`. Unapplied or invalid edits visibly block amount-dependent actions, and the server independently validates every applied value and re-caps it against live balances or inventory. Account, Market, and Trade charts cycle through 30-day, 90-day, one-year, and all-history ranges; retained market, commodity, and personal histories are bounded at five economic years.

## Village Bank and Exchange generation

The first time a player loads an Overworld village, the server searches 48 deterministic positions, reaching as far as 82 blocks from the stable center, for a natural, dry, loaded, and unoccupied bank plot within the four-block foundation tolerance. The player is only the discovery trigger: once a settlement record exists, its persisted stable center drives Bank identity and site selection even while the player explores nearby outskirts. Safe lots sampled wholly beyond vanilla village POI influence rank before interior lots and then nearest-first; equal-distance lots prefer placing the fixed north-facing entrance toward the settlement. Mud and thin snow are rejected as structural support. The floor sits above the highest sampled surface; deterministic biome-matched foundations bridge only bounded air gaps and stop when they meet sound natural ground. A three-block-wide landing and top stair meet the doorway, and additional three-wide stair rows are added only when the bounded approach reaches natural grade safely. Each lane is planned from its own terrain samples and all three must resolve to the same rows, so a cross-slope cannot leave one edge floating. The complete approach and its headroom are validated with the lot rather than lowering the building into higher terrain. The footprint and margin must contain no solid construction or block entities; harmless replaceable vegetation or snow may be cleared by the authored build, but paths, farmland, and protection-claimed blocks reject the candidate. The bank uses plains, desert, savanna, snowy, or taiga palettes. Its version-5 civic blueprint includes a shaped front dormer, glazed bell cupola, articulated ridge, deep green-window bays, paved forecourt wings, a rear ledger pediment, a sheltered portico, a real door and transom, one Exchange Desk, chest and chiseled-bookshelf teller cabinetry, an iron-framed two-wide secure room with an offset Ender Chest and illuminated ledger shelf, a crafting table, layered lighting, and a persistent Banker. Desert uses carved red sandstone and shade, Savanna balances acacia with neutral masonry, Taiga emphasizes moss and stone, Snowy pairs pale cold trim and a snow-aware crown with dark timber, and Plains replaces broad brown wall planes with civic masonry. The Exchange Desk is the only villager job-site authored by a new bank, so its counter cannot accidentally create a row of fishermen. Legacy barrel counter frames remain recognizable for old saves but are never added to new banks. Intact version-2, version-3, and version-4 Banks are always audited against their own frozen plans and never receive version-5 geometry in place.

Before any current Bank dialect is accepted, exact block-light validation sets skylight to zero and examines the complete authored plan rather than only the teller hall. Every roof-covered floor whose state Minecraft accepts as Zombie-spawn support and whose two standing cells are clear—including porch, apron, and three-wide landing cells—must receive block light 7 or greater. The visible fixture plan uses supported natural lights, including a centered pendant under the portico roof.

Before writing, the server preflights every proposed bank block through `VillageDevelopmentProtection.register(PlacementGuard)`. A veto or thrown guard exception denies the site. Each accepted `setBlock` is then compared with the resulting world state. Any failed build rolls back matching authored block types, including panes or fences changed by neighbor updates, and is not recorded as generated. A protected or failed candidate remains transient because the mod cannot claim ownership after an uncertain write, and that path creates no untracked Banker. When no candidate has been proven—whether the loaded scan was complete or not—the Banker-only state, anchor, and village ownership are committed before its canonical Banker is adopted or spawned. While a player remains within 192 horizontal blocks, one bounded recovery pass continues even outside the vanilla village boundary or after the center chunk unloads and expands to 96 loaded-only positions reaching 128 blocks. A successful atomic build replaces fallback provenance with the real anchor, moves or rebinds the same Banker to its Exchange Desk when loaded, and never repairs or clears an old player structure. Bank blocks and that economy save remain subject to the documented cross-file crash limit. Formats 10 and earlier never infer fallback status from center-anchor equality, so ambiguous legacy, damaged, or crash-interrupted Bank markers are not rebuilt. Claim and protection mods must register a guard for their rules to participate.

Generation is discovery-based rather than injected into vanilla jigsaw pools. This supports existing worlds, keeps Fabric and NeoForge aligned, and reduces conflict with world-generation mods.

## Banker safety and identity

The mod registers a real Banker villager profession and a craftable, directional Exchange Desk block and point of interest on both Fabric and NeoForge. Its renewable, early-survival recipe uses one emerald, one book, two leather, and any three planks; obtaining an emerald or book unlocks it in the recipe book. A desk broken normally drops itself, so the generated Bank workstation can be replaced without waiting for the village to rebuild it. The desk uses a layered furniture model with a matching 13.5/16-block selection and collision height, faces the player when placed, and appears under Creative inventory's Functional Blocks tab and search. Unemployed villagers can naturally claim it, and both a naturally employed Banker and a player-placed Exchange Desk open the dashboard. When they do not carry a generated-bank identity, the Village and Fund pages use the nearest managed settlement in the same dimension within 160 blocks; the player's global financial pages remain available when no managed settlement is nearby. An Exchange Desk surviving at an active but unsafe generated Bank deliberately falls back to this personal access while its scoped Banker remains suspended. Replacing the only missing workstation restores managed access when every other authored cell is intact. Legacy tagged Bankers using the librarian profession are migrated when safely eligible, while lectern counters remain interactive only at persisted Overworld legacy-bank locations.

Fallback Banker selection uses only an untouched adult villager with no profession, XP, custom name, or established trades, or an equally untouched villager that naturally claimed an Exchange Desk. Otherwise the mod spawns a new Banker. Existing player-developed villagers are never repurposed. Managed Bankers use vanilla career-lock semantics and remember the generated Exchange Desk as their job site, preventing the Banker appearance from reverting immediately or after an ordinary work cycle.

Bankers are associated with persisted bank identity and the surrounding stable settlement record. Existing grid keys and bank anchors are honored for upgraded worlds; when a second stable village occupies an already-owned legacy grid, it receives a deterministic village-derived key rather than sharing that bank. The same scoped key selects Banker replacement eligibility, Village-page data, and the target of Prosperity Fund and restoration contributions. Each generated Bank key stores one canonical Banker UUID and the last Bank anchor durably assigned to it. The mod does not clear that identity merely because the entity is in an unloaded chunk, and therefore does not create a duplicate; an explicit death is required. Infection and cure assign new Minecraft entity UUIDs, but exact predecessor-lineage markers and save-ordered atomic handoffs preserve the one logical Banker without authorizing a replacement. When a severely demolished Bank is replaced and its old scoped Banker is loaded, that same villager is moved, synchronously saved at the destination, rebound to the new Exchange Desk, and then marked assigned to the replacement. A villager deliberately moved away from both sites is not forcibly teleported. Banker replacement is suppressed while the settlement is Extinct or Abandoned. Player financial accounts are global, so losing a Banker or village never destroys player wealth.

Older saves do not contain per-block ownership for generated banks. A narrowly signature-guarded retrofit recognizes the supported 13x11 Bank only when its persisted anchor, recognized bank workstation (Exchange Desk or scoped legacy lectern), counter frame, doorway, threshold, roof mount, and hanging roof lantern all match. For an unversioned match, it samples the original natural approach lane by lane, preflights the complete three-wide staircase and shallow footing additions, consults protection guards, and writes only into air or replaceable cells. Minecraft's synchronous chunk-save barrier runs before the independent version-2 marker is committed, and `/save-off` defers the work. A thrown barrier error or marker-save failure rolls newly placed entrance cells back, and failed retries are paced; individual Minecraft chunk-write reporting and the cross-file limit remain as documented. Surviving authored green panes can have only their connection properties recalculated, while missing or differently colored panes and other furnishings are never recreated. If the signature or old bank counter is missing, the mod does not rebuild over the site or guess which nearby blocks belong to it; it preserves account access by restoring an eligible fallback Banker when the village lifecycle allows it.

## Village Prosperity structures

The ten physical prosperity projects are Cottage, House, Village Inn, Warehouse, Mine Entrance, Market Square, Smithy, Granary, Guard Post, and Exchange Hall. They are not spawned instantly after offline catch-up. Once approved, a project enters the bounded visual queue, breaks ground, and reveals more of its authored structure in proportion to Planning. The final block and operational completion still wait for 100 percent Planning, and every placement remains paced, loaded-chunk-only, and player-protected.

Candidate sites must be loaded, naturally surfaced, dry, clear, and within four blocks of height variation. Each project inspects a deterministic 64-site sequence extending to 84 blocks from the stable village center, one candidate per construction pulse, without loading chunks. Project floors are placed above the highest surface rather than excavating it, while deterministic foundation cells bridge bounded drops and stop at sound natural ground. Replaceable plants or snow may be cleared, but solid construction, block entities, paths, farmland, and protection-vetoed cells reject the candidate. Unsafe candidates are skipped before reservation. Once a site is persisted, an ordinary later solid, protected, or unloaded placement defers construction with exponential backoff and retains the exact reservation—even at a recorded zero prefix. A process crash between the economy cursor write and Minecraft's independent chunk write can still leave the cursor ahead; the integrity pass treats that ambiguity as damage instead of auto-repairing a block the player may have removed. Housing and production effects remain inactive in visual mode until the authored structure is physically verified complete.

A low-frequency integrity pass audits one completed project at a time, and versioned generated Banks receive an equivalent full-plan check. Any missing or replaced authored block suspends the scoped Banker's operation immediately. Ordinary partial edits are never regenerated and do not create a duplicate; restoring the complete authored plan lets a later audit reactivate the building. Safe replaceable, dry, block-entity-free vegetation or snow in authored outdoor clearance does not count as structural damage. While an active Bank is unsafe, its surviving Exchange Desk remains usable as unscoped personal banking so the player's finances are never stranded. Severe demolition requires both 12 mismatches and 30 percent of authored structure cells. It retires the old lot or Bank anchor and queues the same village need at a different safe site, leaving the player's edits or ruins untouched. Every retired project bound and Bank anchor remains excluded through a bounded append-only history of up to 16 entries; when that cap is full, further damage remains unsafe instead of forgetting an older exclusion. Exchange Desks at retired Bank coordinates are not reinterpreted as personal desks. Safe append-only template upgrades still use the ordinary guarded construction queue, and solid construction, block entities, protected placements, and unloaded chunks are never overwritten or force-loaded. Trails are deliberately non-authoritative: dirt path and gravel form the center while coarse dirt edges both shoulders. They connect the entrance to the nearest earlier completed-project branch or a stable village-edge hub, adopt an existing TES-style route, and skip protected, occupied, or non-terrain cells without suspending the building. After an older modular route finishes its frozen plan, a separate versioned one-time scan visits every historically coarse center coordinate without reusing its construction cursor. A claim guard is required to distinguish player-placed natural-ground blocks from the same block types generated by Minecraft.

Lighting needed to pass the exact gate is structural safety, not renewable decoration. Blueprint V2 derives required emitters and their chains, terminal beams, or mounting blocks from the cumulative stages while leaving its canonical hash unchanged; every versioned Bank light and support is already part of its full authored plan. Removing or replacing one makes the project manual-restoration-required or the Bank unsafe. Neither path repairs the player's change; restoring the exact authored cells is what permits a later integrity audit to reactivate the building.

Cottages, Houses, and Inns contain real beds and clear entry paths, and Warehouses use chests rather than barrels. Blueprint V2 supplies 52 immutable whole-building gold masters across the ten roles: six Cottage plans, six House plans, and five plans for every other role. The nine-small, 18-medium, 14-large, and 11-landmark mix ranges from compact roadside buildings to orchard estates, tower courts, courtyard inns, warehouse basilicas, silo complexes, foundries, quarries, bazaars, citadels, and a monumental bourse. Each revision owns its floorplan, roof, frontage, circulation, functional interior, and architectural detail as one reviewed structure rather than mixing those pieces procedurally. Connected framed eaves and covered entries use the village's biome dialect, Market Squares have lit stalls, Guard Posts have defensive works, mines have functional timber machinery, and production or civic buildings contain a bounded set of theme-appropriate utility and job-site blocks. A deterministic tier-aware shuffle bag favors smaller plans early, unlocks large plans at tier 2 and landmarks at tier 4, balances eligible templates within each scale, and avoids an immediate repeat when another exists; semantic material palettes, dressing kits, and descriptor-approved mirroring add safe visual variation without changing the structure. Town and city stages remain append-only, and a later tier decline never removes an installed layer. Those vanilla workstations give residents ordinary reasons to visit the new district; they do not install custom AI or change the simulation's economic authority. During active placement, at most two suitable nearby residents may receive an occasional one-shot, low-speed navigation request toward a safe exterior waypoint, then look, swing, and emit a small particle. This theatre remains bounded.

## Interaction safety

The server remains authoritative for all actions. Sell-all, CD closure, lending funding, and Fund contributions require the same risky action to be submitted twice within a short server-owned confirmation window. Changing the selection or allowing the window to expire cancels it; a client cannot bypass it by locally changing a button label. The menu closes when the player dies, is removed, or leaves the allowed interaction range. A configurable cooldown prevents duplicate button and packet spam.

## Configuration

The world `data/the_emerald_standard-config.properties` file controls onboarding, bank generation, transaction throttling, and Village Prosperity. See the [complete configuration reference](CONFIGURATION.md) for every setting, bound, and dependency. The four key prosperity switches are:

```properties
village_prosperity.simulation_enabled=true
village_prosperity.visual_progression_enabled=true
village_prosperity.market_integration_enabled=true
village_prosperity.automatic_recovery_enabled=true
village_prosperity.donations_enabled=true
village_prosperity.endowments_enabled=true
village_prosperity.project_sponsorship_enabled=true
village_prosperity.targeted_donations_enabled=true
village_prosperity.donor_recognition_enabled=true
village_prosperity.fast_track_capital_enabled=true
village_prosperity.endowment_annual_payout_bps=400
village_prosperity.minimum_emergency_reserve_percent=20
village_prosperity.max_monthly_treasury_spending=24
```

The Fund settings independently enable contribution features and recognition, choose the protected endowment's annual payout, reserve a fraction of ordinary grants for emergencies, and cap routine releases into the simulated local economy. With fast-track capital enabled, a dedicated Project Sponsorship, matching-purpose Direct Grant balance, and then General player-origin capital may atomically cover the current project's exact input gap and remaining labor. The full remainder must be available or nothing is debited. Passive Endowment payout and emergency reserves remain in the capped channel; extra funds remain untouched and physical construction stays paced and protected.

Administrators can inspect or reload configuration with `/emerald config show` and `/emerald config reload`.
