# V1 test checklist

1. Launch Minecraft 26.2 with exactly one loader JAR.
2. Run `/emerald help` and `/emerald market`.
3. Give yourself emeralds, deposit them, buy VILX, and confirm `/emerald portfolio` changes.
4. Test savings and a CD.
5. Fund a villager loan. Confirm there is no borrow command and no negative-balance path.
6. Exchange each supported valuable resource.
7. Save and quit. Reopen after at least 20 real minutes and confirm the economic day and prices advanced.
8. Reopen immediately and confirm the same saved market is retained rather than rerolled.
9. Test multiplayer with two accounts and confirm balances remain UUID-specific while market prices are shared.
