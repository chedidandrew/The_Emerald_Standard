# Economy model

The Emerald Standard uses deterministic seeded pseudo-random daily returns. Regimes persist probabilistically, which creates volatility clustering and multi-day bull, recession, crash, and recovery periods. VILX is the market factor. Individual companies combine market beta, a small long-run alpha assumption, and idiosyncratic volatility.

Rates are targets and distributions, never guaranteed returns. Savings and CDs accrue daily. Villager loans are investments made by the player and cannot create player debt.

Offline catch-up uses elapsed wall-clock time at 20 real minutes per Minecraft economic day. Game-time progression is also recognized, and the larger elapsed count is used to avoid double-counting ordinary online play.
