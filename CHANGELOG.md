# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-31
- Initial public V1 architecture for Minecraft 26.2.
- Added deterministic persistent market simulation and economic regimes.
- Added VILX and eight Minecraft-themed equities.
- Added savings, CDs, player-funded villager loans, and valuable-resource exchange.
- Added offline catch-up and micro-emerald accounting.
- Explicitly prohibited player borrowing and debt at the data-model level.
- Added Fabric and NeoForge loader projects and CI build workflow.
